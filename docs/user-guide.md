# User guide

The NIT Arunachal Pradesh Alumni Network is a private, verified community for the institute's students,
alumni, faculty and staff. Everyone you meet here has been checked by the institute, so you can trust who
you are talking to.

## Getting started

1. **Sign up** with your name, email and a password, then confirm your email from the link we send you.
2. **Get verified.**
   - **Institute email** (for example a student address): you are verified as soon as you confirm it.
   - **No institute email** (most alumni): fill in the verification form with your department, degree,
     graduation year and roll number. A person in the alumni office reviews it, and you get an email with
     the decision.
3. **Complete your profile.** Add a photo, headline, work history and skills so classmates can find you.

Until you are verified you can sign in and edit your basic profile, but you can't use the network yet.

## What you can do

- **Profile:** your headline, bio, location, work history, extra education, skills and links, with a
  photo. You choose who sees each part (see [Privacy](#privacy)).
- **Directory:** search members by name, company, skill, department or graduation year.
- **Connections:** send a connection request from someone's profile. If a request is declined, you can ask
  again after 30 days. You can block anyone; they won't be able to find or message you, and they aren't
  told.
- **Messages:** private 1:1 chats and group conversations of up to 20 people. New messages arrive
  instantly. You can report a message that breaks the rules.
- **Mentorship:** alumni can offer to mentor and set how many mentees they take. Students browse mentors
  and send a request saying what they need help with. The mentor accepts or declines, then marks the
  mentorship started and completed.
- **Feed:** share posts with images and a link, comment and react. Announcements from the institute are
  pinned at the top.
- **Achievements:** alumni and faculty can submit an award, publication, promotion or similar. Once the
  alumni office approves it, it's published to the feed.
- **Jobs and internships:** browse openings and apply on the employer's site. Alumni can post openings;
  they appear after the placement team approves them and close automatically at the deadline.
- **Events:** browse reunions, talks and workshops and register for a seat. The organizer sees who
  registered and marks who attended; if an event is cancelled, everyone registered is notified.
- **Donations:** support institute campaigns. You pledge an amount, pay by bank transfer or cheque using
  the instructions on the campaign page, then add your payment reference so the alumni office can confirm
  it.
- **Notifications:** the bell shows what happened (requests, replies, approvals, event changes). Choose
  which kinds also reach your email in notification settings. Account and security emails always arrive.

## Privacy

Your profile has a visibility level:

| Level            | Who can see it                                |
| ---------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Everyone         | Anyone, including people who aren't signed in |
| Verified members | Any verified member (the default)             |
| Connections      | Only people you're connected with             |
| Only me          | Nobody else                                   |

You can make contact details, location, experience and education stricter than the rest of your profile,
never looser. Your email, phone and roll number are never shown to other members. If you hide your profile,
people who can't see it get "not found", so they can't even tell it exists.

Administrators can open any profile for support or safety reasons, and every time they do it's recorded.

## Roles

Each person has one or more roles. A role is a bundle of permissions: it decides what you can do, not who
you are. Roles exist so that each kind of responsibility goes to the people who should hold it, and nobody
gets more power than their job needs.

### Members

Every role below includes the member features: profile, directory, connections, messages, feed, events,
donations, notifications and reporting content.

| Role        | Who                      | Why it exists, and what it adds                                                                               |
| ----------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Student** | Current students         | Students come here for guidance and opportunities. They can **request mentorship** from alumni.               |
| **Alumni**  | Verified former students | Alumni are the heart of the network. They can **become mentors**, **post jobs**, and **submit achievements**. |
| **Faculty** | Teaching staff           | Faculty stay in touch with former students and can **submit achievements**.                                   |
| **Staff**   | Institute staff          | Staff take part as members; extra duties are granted individually when needed.                                |

### Moderation and administration

| Role                   | Why it exists                                                                   | What it can do                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Moderator**          | Keeps the community safe without access to accounts or settings                 | Review reports, remove posts and comments, hide messages (reading a reported message is recorded), view the user list                                                                               |
| **Alumni Coordinator** | The alumni office: decides who is a verified alumnus and runs alumni activities | Approve or reject verification requests, review achievements, create and manage events, publish announcements, run donation campaigns, view analytics for its area, give out some extra permissions |
| **T&P Admin**          | The Training and Placement cell owns career opportunities                       | Approve or reject job posts, manage any job, create events, view career analytics                                                                                                                   |
| **Institute Admin**    | Runs the platform day to day on the institute's behalf                          | Everything above, plus suspend and reactivate accounts, assign roles (except admin roles), see all donations, read the audit log, replay failed emails                                              |
| **Super Admin**        | A small technical owner group for the system itself                             | Everything, including system settings (data retention) and assigning admin roles                                                                                                                    |

**Chapter admin** is not a role but a set of permissions limited to one alumni chapter (its events, posts,
reports and announcements). The permission system supports it; chapters themselves aren't available yet.

### Why the roles are split this way

- **Trust:** only the alumni office can verify alumni, so the directory stays trustworthy.
- **Least privilege:** a moderator can remove a harmful post but can't suspend accounts or change
  settings. Each role gets only what its job needs.
- **Checks and balances:** nobody can review their own request, job, achievement or report, change their
  own roles, or grant a permission they don't hold. The last super admin can't be removed.
- **Accountability:** every decision an admin makes (verifications, role changes, suspensions, moderation)
  is written to an audit log that can't be edited.

## Account states

| State       | Meaning                                                                                                 |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Pending     | Signed up, not yet verified. You can edit your basic profile and submit verification.                   |
| Verified    | Full access according to your roles.                                                                    |
| Rejected    | Your verification wasn't accepted. You can resubmit; after three rejections, contact the alumni office. |
| Suspended   | Your account is on hold. You can sign in to see your status but can't do anything else.                 |
| Deactivated | The account is closed and can't sign in.                                                                |

## Getting help

For verification questions or a suspended account, contact the alumni office (details on the Contact
page). To report harmful content, use **Report** on the post, comment or message.
