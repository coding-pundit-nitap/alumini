# R-11 — Email provider failing

**Alert:** `EmailSendFailing` (ticket: more than 20 % of `email.send` attempts retried or failed over 15 minutes, at least 20 attempts).

## What it means

SMTP sends are failing. Retries continue with backoff; users still get in-app notifications. Verification and password-reset emails are delayed, which blocks sign-up and recovery.

## Check first

1. The provider's status page and account (quota, suspended sender, credentials rotated?).
2. Worker logs: `job.retry` for `email.send` — the stored reason is the SMTP code, never the address.
3. **Grafana → Async:** "Job outcomes per second" for `email.send`; dead `email.send` jobs.
4. Auth flows: are verification/reset emails among the failures?

## Mitigate

- Provider outage → wait; retries resume on their own. If prolonged, switch `SMTP_URL` to the fallback provider and restart the worker.
- Credentials/quota → fix and restart the worker.
- Show a banner that verification and reset emails are delayed.
- Once fixed: dead `email.send` jobs → `pnpm worker:cli jobs:retry --queue email --all`; failed notification emails → [R-15](./R-15.md).

## Escalate

Password reset unavailable for more than 1 hour → SEV-2.

## Resolved when

The failure ratio is under 5 % for 30 minutes.
