# R-14 — Upload scan backlog

**Alerts:** `UploadScanBacklog` (ticket).

## What it means

An upload has waited more than 30 minutes, for 30 minutes, between upload and the malware scan's verdict (`PENDING_SCAN`). Until it is scanned, the file is unusable: a member's new profile photo does not appear. The pipeline fails closed, so nothing unscanned is ever served.

## Check first

1. Grafana → Async: `upload_scan_pending`, and the `upload-scan` queue's waiting and failed counts.
2. Is clamd healthy? `docker compose ps clamav`. After a restart it needs a few minutes to load signatures, and it holds about 1 GB of RAM.
3. Worker logs: `docker compose logs --since 1h worker | grep upload`. Look for scan timeouts, `ECONNREFUSED` to `clamav:3310`, or storage errors.
4. Is the worker processing at all? `WorkerDown` or `QueueBacklogGrowing` would point at [R-3](./R-3.md).

## Mitigate

- clamd down or out of memory → `docker compose restart clamav`. Add memory or swap if it was OOM-killed.
- Worker stuck → `docker compose restart worker`. Scans are retried from the queue.
- Dead scan jobs → replay them after the cause is fixed ([R-4](./R-4.md)).
- **Do not bypass scanning.** Files stay unusable until they are scanned.

## Escalate

Backlog older than 4 hours → SEV-3 communication to members (reliability §10.5).

## Resolved when

`upload_scan_oldest_pending_age_seconds` is back under a few minutes and `upload_scan_pending` drains.
