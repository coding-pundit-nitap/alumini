# R-9 — Auth abuse and permission denials

**Alerts:** `LoginFailureSpike`, `AdminPermissionDenialSpike`, `AuthzDenialAuditFailing` (ticket + security review).

## What it means

- `LoginFailureSpike`: more than 50 email sign-ins failed (wrong password) or were rate limited in 10 minutes. It is either credential stuffing against member accounts, or a broken login after a release. The threshold is a starting value: after the first month of production, set it from the observed baseline (Grafana → Auth & security, `auth_sign_in_total`).
- `AdminPermissionDenialSpike`: more than 20 denied checks on admin-tier permissions (user management, roles, audit, system settings, notification replay, analytics, donations, reported messages) in 10 minutes. Someone is probing admin endpoints, or a release broke a role's grants.
- `AuthzDenialAuditFailing`: denied admin actions are not being written to the audit log — an SRS-critical control has failed.

## Check first

1. **Grafana → Auth & security:** sign-ins by outcome; which permissions are denied, and since when. Many `invalid_credentials` and few `success` across many accounts means stuffing. A sudden drop to no `success` at all means login itself is broken.
2. Audit log (admin → audit): who was denied — one account, many accounts, one source?
3. Logs: `event="authz.denied"` (permission, `user_id`, `request_id` on each line).
4. Did a deploy or a role change just happen? A broken grant shows as denials for real staff accounts.
5. For audit failures: logs `authz.denied.audit_failed` warnings and PostgreSQL health ([R-5](./R-5.md)).

## Mitigate

- Credential stuffing → block the source ranges at Nginx, lower the sign-in rate limit, and force password resets (deleting sessions) for any account that signed in from those sources.
- Login broken after a release → roll back (reliability §8.5).
- Probing from member accounts → tighten rate limits, suspend the accounts, block the source ranges at Nginx.
- Broken grants after a release → roll back or restore the role's permissions (audited).
- Audit writes failing → fix the database cause; the denials themselves are still enforced.

## Escalate

Any successful takeover, or admin-tier denials coming from a real admin's account → reliability §10.6 (suspected compromise).

## Resolved when

Denials are back at baseline and the audit counters stop increasing.
