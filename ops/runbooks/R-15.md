# R-15 — Failed notification emails

**Alert:** `FailedEmailDeliveries` (ticket: any notification email marked `FAILED` in the last 30 minutes).

## What it means

A notification email failed permanently (a 5xx from the provider) or on its last retry. The in-app notification was delivered; only the email is missing. Each failure is a `FAILED` EMAIL row on the notification, counted by `notification_delivery_failed_total{channel="EMAIL"}`.

## Check first

1. **Grafana → Async:** "Notification emails failed (30m)" by category. A burst across categories points at the provider ([R-11](./R-11.md)); a trickle is usually individual bad addresses.
2. **Admin → Failed emails** (`/admin/notifications`): the failed deliveries, their category and the stored SMTP code.
3. SMTP code 550/551/553 → the address does not exist. These should not be replayed; they are suppressed after bounces.

## Mitigate

1. Fix the cause first (provider, credentials, quota — see [R-11](./R-11.md)).
2. Replay from **Admin → Failed emails**: each replay re-queues that notification's email job and writes a `notification.replay` audit entry. The API equivalent is `POST /api/v1/admin/notifications/replay` with `{ "notificationId": "<uuid>" }` (needs `notification.replay`).
3. A replay answering `409 REPLAY_JOB_GONE` means BullMQ already dropped the job (7-day retention); the in-app notification stands.
4. If logs show `notification.replay.audit_failed`: the email was re-queued but the audit row is missing. Record the replay manually in the incident notes with the notification id, actor and time, and check PostgreSQL ([R-5](./R-5.md)).

## Resolved when

The replayed deliveries show `SENT` and the counter stops increasing.
